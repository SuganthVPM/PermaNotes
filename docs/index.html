What’s new in v1.7.0

Images and screenshots

Paste a screenshot or drag a PNG, JPEG, BMP, or GIF into a note. Resize it beside your text, double-click to zoom and pan, or right-click to copy, export, or save the original.

Smaller previews save memory while you work. PermaNotes loads the original detail when you need it.

Automatic links

Type or paste a URL to turn it into a clickable link. Click to open or copy it, or Ctrl-click to open it directly.

Welcome tour and help

Take the welcome tour, see what’s new after an update, or open Help & Features from a note’s right-click menu.

Dark Settings

Choose default note colors and opacity, set a storage location, and manage startup in the redesigned Settings window.

Made for your desktop

Notes sit on the desktop layer beneath open windows. You can pin individual notes above other windows or let clicks pass through them.

Editing
Rich text, live Markdown, interactive checklists, and shared Undo for text and images.
Organization
Search across notes, schedule Windows reminders, and manage open and closed notes.
Privacy
Lock notes against edits or hide them from supported screen capture.
Storage
Notes auto-save with recovery backups. Notes and images move together when you change folders.
